How customer records were reconciled

Method applied separately to each store

Names and addresses are compared after case, punctuation, and whitespace normalization; phones are compared by digits; e-mails are compared case-insensitively. Two records are merged when any three non-empty normalized fields match. All user IDs in a merged identity are retained in the best-buyer output. Exact duplicate orders are removed, invalid order rows are excluded, and euro prices are converted at €1 = $1.20.
